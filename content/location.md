Apple Cottage lies on Silver Street in the village of Creetown (DG8 7HU). Nestled between the rugged hills of Cairnsmore of Fleet and the tidal estuary of Wigtown Bay, Creetown offers a peaceful rural setting with a strong community. The local primary school, community centre and village shop are all within easy walking distance, while the A75 provides fast access to Newton Stewart, the Galloway Forest Park and the wider Dumfries & Galloway coastline. Trails, beaches and woodland walks are on your doorstep — making this an ideal base for families, outdoor enthusiasts and those seeking a healthy work‑life balance.

### Local Highlights

- Creetown Heritage Museum — volunteer‑run collection of local cultural and industrial history (~1 min drive)
- Gem Rock Museum & Café — gemstones, crystals and fossils with a family‑friendly café (~2 min drive)
- Kirroughtree Visitor Centre & 7stanes — biking and forest trails with café and bike shop (~5 min drive)
- Ellangowan Hotel (The Wicker Man pub) — friendly local with film memorabilia (in village, ~1–2 min drive)
- Cairnsmore of Fleet NNR — wild granite hill with panoramic views and wildlife (~15 min drive)
- Mossyard Beach — small sandy bay on Fleet Bay, great for family days and small craft (~15 min drive)

### Travel & Connectivity

- A75: ~2 min
- Kirroughtree: ~5 min
- Newton Stewart: ~10 min
- Mossyard: ~15 min
